LiSTd for Mac

For Intel and Apple Silicon Macs running macOS 14 or newer.
No Python, Homebrew, Sockseek or Rosetta installation is needed.

1. Drag LiSTd.app into Applications, then open it.
2. Sign in with your own Soulseek username and password. Close other Soulseek
   clients using that account. LiSTd saves the password in this Mac's Keychain.
3. Import or drop a CSV/text tracklist. Check artist/title order and quality.
   With automatic downloads enabled, importing starts downloading and organizing.

FIRST OPEN
This build is not Apple-notarized. After trying to open it, go to System Settings
> Privacy & Security > Open Anyway for LiSTd, then confirm Open. A managed work
Mac may prohibit this. Do not disable Gatekeeper or other macOS protections.

YOUR FILES
Downloads and import history live in ~/Music/LiSTd.
The numerical analysis cache lives in ~/Library/Caches/LiSTd/numba.
Your account and music are not included in this archive.
The account button changes the account or signs out. Signing out keeps your music.
Playback of local files works without signing in.

Use the Skin menu for Amped, Silverwave, Original or Signal / Dual Deck.
Beat/tempo estimates can need manual correction; sync is a one-time alignment.
